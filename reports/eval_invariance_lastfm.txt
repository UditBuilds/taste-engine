not rediscovery: --test-end and --rediscovery/--both are mutually exclusive
in evaluate.py's argparse (added in f88a160, "Pin the replay evaluation
window with --test-end") - passing both is a parser.error, not a silent
fallback. The brief's SECTION 6 says "the rediscovery figures must be
byte-identical," which command A alone cannot check. Command B is added to
actually cover that: --both --test-days 30 is the exact invariance-check
form CLAUDE.md's own State section already used for the backfill toggle
check ("re-ran --both --test-days 30, restored, ran again - byte-identical
output both times"), reused here rather than invented, and it exercises both
replay and rediscovery in one run. This discrepancy between the brief's
literal command and its stated intent is reported rather than silently
resolved one way.
