Why only cluster_diverse: it is the one strategy in evaluate.py/recommend.py
that reads embedding-based clustering output at all (`cluster_tracks`,
needed to diversify picks across clusters). score, most_played, and recency
are pure play-count/recency computations with no path to the embedding text
that Part B changed for 2 of 2,918 tracks - they could not have moved, and
the measurement confirms they did not, to the byte.

Magnitude, for scale against the underlying clustering delta
(reports/embedding_modes_remeasured.md: title_artist ARI moved -0.0031,
~0.5% relative): cluster_diverse's replay ndcg@50 moved ~-4.1% relative
(0.3213->0.3080); its rediscovery mean_ndcg@20 moved ~-0.8% relative
(0.2587->0.2567). Neither headline conclusion in README §2 (replay: score
beats most_played by +1.6%; rediscovery: recency beats most_played by
+143.6%) depends on cluster_diverse, and neither moved.
