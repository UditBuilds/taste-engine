# Privacy Policy — taste-engine

taste-engine is a personal, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses the author's own YouTube account to read playlists and video
metadata, and to create playlists in that same account.

All data is stored locally on the author's machine. No data is collected,
transmitted to any third party, sold, or shared. There are no other users.
